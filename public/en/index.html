FAQ

What people usually ask us.

If yours is missing, write to us — actual people answer.

Do I have to connect my bank?

No. You log entries by speaking or typing and Hera works out the rest. It works exactly the same if you deal in cash, which is why there's no bank login to set up.

Can I log expenses by voice or with a photo?

Both. Dictate the entry and Hera understands it, or photograph the receipt and the AI reads the amount, the description and the category. Then it shows you what it understood so you can confirm.

What does Hera remember about me?

Your goals, your spending habits and the decisions you already made. That memory lives in readable text files you can review, export or delete at any time.

Can I correct it when it gets something wrong?

Always. Hera confirms before saving an entry, and you can edit any detail afterwards. When you correct it, it learns from the correction.

Is it free?

You can start free, with no credit card and no ads. If a paid plan arrives later, it will be for extra features — never to get your own data back.

Does it work on a bad connection?

Logging and reviewing your information needs very little bandwidth. The AI answers do need a connection, and they're saved for the next time you open the app.
